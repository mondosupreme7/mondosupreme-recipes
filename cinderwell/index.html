Skirmish

Cinderwell

Two banners over a drowned basin. Gather ore and thatch, raise a hearth, and break the other keep.

Tap to select. Drag a box around your own troops. Tap the ground to move, or to attack-move an army. Two fingers, or a drag from the screen edge, pans the basin.

Controls
  • Phone: the command card under the map has at most six actions. Idle finds a hand with nothing to do. Army selects every fighter.
  • Desktop: drag a box, right-click to command, arrows pan, wheel zooms. S stop, H hold, A attack-move, F idle hands, Q whole army, Space centers, 1–6 press the card, Esc cancels, M menu.
  • Pikes chew rams. Bows pick off pikes if they keep the range. Rams crack buildings. Kilnward’s Furnace Guard shrugs off arrows. Reedhost’s Mist Skiff runs the river down and hunts rams.
  • The shrine unlocks the ram and the mythic, plus a whetstone. Destroy the enemy hearth to win. Progress stays in this browser.

Pixel sprites are drawn for Cinderwell in this page. No outside art.
