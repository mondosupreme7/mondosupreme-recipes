Cinderwell

Gather ore and thatch, raise a hearth, break the other keep.

Tap to select. Drag a box. Tap ground to move. Two fingers pan.

Controls
  • Tap a building, tap the map to preview, then Place. Red ground will not hold it. By hearth previews a spot beside home. Idle finds a free hand. Army selects every fighter. Tap the under-attack line to look there.
  • Desktop: right-click commands, arrows pan, wheel zooms. S stop, H hold, A attack-move, F idle, Q army, N preview by the Hearth, P place, Esc cancels.
  • Pikes chew rams. Bows pick off pikes. Rams crack buildings. Guards shrug off arrows. Skiffs cross the river. Pixel sprites are drawn in this page.
