Swing Trading Rules
  1. Trade with the trend, buying pullbacks above the 200-day average.
  2. Only enter on a clear setup.
  3. Avoid holding through earnings.
  4. Plan your entry, stop, and target before you buy.
  5. Take only trades with at least 2:1 reward-to-risk.
  6. Risk 1–2% of your account per trade at most.
  7. Always use a stop and never lower it.
  8. Take partial profits and trail the rest.
  9. Exit if the trade stalls for 5–10 days.
  10. Never average down.
  11. No emotional, FOMO, or revenge trades.
  12. Journal and review every trade.
