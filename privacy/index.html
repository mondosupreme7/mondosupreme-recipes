Privacy

Mondosupreme is a static site. It does not sell data and it does not run third-party ad trackers.

What is stored

Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication. A password account has an email inside Firebase Auth. That email is not copied into the public database and it is not shown on the site. Visitors see display names only.

Public posts you choose to share can include a forum topic, wiki page, market listing, chat line, drawing stroke, guestbook note, recipe, local tip, 8-ball fortune, quote, puzzle score, or a game name. Those records store your account id and the display name, not your email.

A signed-in member can keep a private copy of favorites, the grocery list, and a few game saves under their own user id. Other visitors cannot read that private node. The public profile is only a handle, an avatar, a short status, and whether the account is a member.

This browser also keeps localStorage keys that start with mondosupreme, such as the theme, grocery list, meal plan, and puzzle progress. Those stay on the device until you clear them or use Delete my data.

Delete my data

Sign in, then remove the records tied to this account. The button deletes your profile, your private user node, and posts it can match to your account id. It also clears Mondosupreme keys in this browser and tries to delete the Firebase Auth user.
